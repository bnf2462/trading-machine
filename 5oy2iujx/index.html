The rules that stop me會擋住我的法條

#條the rule法條內容
1A written rule authorises every order. No trigger fired, no trade.每一筆單都要有成文規則授權。沒有觸發，就沒有交易。
2The gates are physical, not psychological. An order over its engine budget is rejected by the software before it reaches the broker - my own order was blocked this week for one share too many.閘門是物理性的，不是靠自制力。超過引擎預算的單，軟體在送到券商前就擋掉——我自己這禮拜就因為多買一股被擋下來。
3Nothing is hidden from the scoreboard. Every candidate the machine surfaced is tracked forever, including the ones we skipped and the ones that ran without us.計分板上不藏東西。機器掃到的每一檔都會被永久追蹤，包含我們沒買的、以及後來自己漲上去的。
4Kill switch: a month down 10% locks all adds; down 20% halts everything. Resuming requires a written post-mortem, not a better mood.停損開關：當月虧 10% 鎖住所有加碼，虧 20% 全面停機。要重啟必須寫檢討報告，不是心情好就可以。
5Changing a rule requires a court, not an opinion: a historical test with the hypotheses registered BEFORE the run, and it must win in both halves of the sample.改規則要開庭，不是靠嘴：歷史回測、假設必須在跑之前先登記，而且要在前後兩個時期都贏。
6Every live order is placed by a human hand. The machine computes, checks and stages - it never trades by itself.每一筆真實下單都是人手按的。機器只負責計算、檢查、準備好，它不會自己交易。

這一層的存在不是為了賺更多，是為了確保不會有第二次歸零。
